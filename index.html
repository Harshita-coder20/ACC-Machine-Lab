Questions people ask

Is anything saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser on your own device.

What are 1, 2 and 3‑address machines?

They are three ways to write the same order. The more places an instruction names, the fewer instructions the job needs. See the Formats page.

Why does the number jump back to 0 after 255?

An 8‑bit machine can only hold 256 values, like a car odometer rolling over.

About the project

Problem

Construct an interpreter for an assembly language on a machine with a single register, the accumulator, where all operands are in memory. Required: ADD, SUB, MUL, DIV, AND, OR, NOT, LD, ST.
